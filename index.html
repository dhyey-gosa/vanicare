A case, from allocation
to closing report.

01

Case allocated

Administrator

An admin registers the patient, opens a case with its disorder profile and required clinical hours, and assigns it to a student therapist.

02

Plan drafted

Therapist

The therapist studies the case, drafts a therapy plan with goals and session structure, and submits it before touching a single session.

03

Plan reviewed

Supervisor

The supervisor approves the plan or returns it with feedback. Nothing moves forward until a qualified clinician signs off.

04

Sessions logged

Therapist

Every session is recorded against the approved plan. Clinical hours accumulate automatically toward the requirement.

05

Report evaluated

Supervisor

The therapist closes the loop with a progress report. The supervisor evaluates outcomes, and the case is ready to close with a complete audit trail.
